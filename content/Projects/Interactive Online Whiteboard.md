Miro like website that myself and 3 others developed Semester 2 2025. Tech stack includes react, node, javascript, supabase, and firebase auth.


Accomplished: 
- Whiteboard tools: Pen, Highlighter, Eraser, Sticky note, undo/redo, clear
- Per board avatar stack of online users
- Live cursors of users on the whiteboard
- Real time collaboration/update of objects and strokes on whiteboard
- Persistance with file management and saving via supabase
- Per board chat room with typing indicator
- Account authentication via firebase (google and email)
- Zoom in and out of 5k by 5k whiteboard
